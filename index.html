    Why uPVC

    Built for heat, rain
    and everyday life.

    uPVC frames are made to last in a tropical climate — and to look good doing it. Multi-chambered profiles, sealed glazing and smooth-running hardware, in a finish that never needs repainting.

    Weather resistant

    uPVC doesn't rust in the rain or rot in the humidity, and it holds its shape in the heat.

    No anay, no rot

    Termites can't eat it and moisture can't rot it — the two biggest problems with wooden frames.

    Low maintenance

    No sanding, varnishing or repainting. A wipe with soap and water keeps frames looking new.

    Modern design

    Slim, clean profiles in white, black or wood finish that suit modern and classic homes alike.

    Quieter, cooler rooms

    Double-glazed glass is available — it generally helps cut outside noise and heat compared with single glass.

    10-year profile warranty

    ARTEM backs its uPVC profiles with a 10-year warranty. Ask for full terms with your quote.
